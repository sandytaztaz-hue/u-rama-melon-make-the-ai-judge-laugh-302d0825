Entrants write a single joke about office life, one line, under 200 characters, and enter it as text. A judge model reads the entries two at a time against a published judge brief and picks the one that gets the bigger laugh, weighing how true the observation is to office life, how surprising the turn is, how tight the line is and how fresh it feels. The best ranked jokes take the rewards.

The program writes one office joke to `/output/entry.txt`: a lunch abandoned in the office fridge since March, which HR now offers to register as a tax dependent. One concrete scene, one real month, one named office actor, and the punchline is the bureaucracy adopting the lunch — fresh against a field crowded with printers, standups, and "we're a family".

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
